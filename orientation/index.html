A structured plan to make you productive, connected, and confident in your role.

Learn & Integrate

Build your foundation. Absorb information, get comfortable, and build relationships.

✓ Set up accounts: Google Workspace, HubSpot CRM, Zoom

✓ Connect with your onboarding buddy for regular check-ins

Move from learning to actively contributing. Time to get your hands dirty.

✓ Take ownership of work assignments appropriate to your role

✓ Share feedback about your onboarding experience
3

Own & Excel

Greater independence and ownership. You're becoming a seasoned team member.

✓ Lead a project or initiative scaled to your experience
✓ Propose improvements or new ideas to our processes

✓ Full integration into team activities and decision-making

AI Solutions Advisor (Inbound Sales & BD)

You're the front line for inbound business. Think advisor + salesperson. Your goal: convert opportunities into impact for clients.

Key Responsibilities

Serve as trusted advisor to potential clients. Understand their challenges and match them with our products and services.
Manage inbound pipeline: qualify leads, conduct demos, follow up, and close deals
Master our product suite (Accredify, Velocity, GovConPro) and services to propose viable AI solutions
Stay informed on industry trends and relay prospect feedback to inform product development

First 90 Days

Shadow senior sales calls and product demos. Learn our sales playbook inside-out
Master HubSpot CRM for tracking leads, logging activities, and generating reports
By weeks 3-4, take the lead on inbound calls with mentor support
Independently handle at least one client inquiry from first call to proposal by Day 60
